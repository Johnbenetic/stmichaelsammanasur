Our Mission & Identity

About Our Parish Community

Rooted in faith and nourished by the Word of God, St. Michael’s Church stands as a sanctuary of hope in Sammanasur.

Sacramental Living

Accompanying individuals and families through Holy Baptism, First Eucharist, Confirmation, Matrimony, and Anointing.

Faith Formation

Providing structured Sunday Catechism for children, youth leadership programs, and adult prayer circles.

Charity & Outreach

Supporting local families in need through community welfare efforts and compassionate charitable initiatives.

Parish Fellowship

Building strong, supportive bonds through vibrant parish groups, choir ministries, and annual feast celebrations.
